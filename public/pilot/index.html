01 Scope

One bounded process. Not an enterprise transformation.

A useful pilot begins where the evidence problem is already visible: an AI-assisted workflow whose inputs, transformations or resulting records may need to be explained later.

The objective is to test whether that workflow can become materially more traceable and reconstructable without replacing the organisation's entire operating environment.

02 Process

Four steps from workflow to evidence package.

01

Choose one workflow

Select a real process where AI-assisted work, documentation or decision support creates a traceability requirement.

02

Define the evidence boundary

Agree which sources, states and resulting artifacts belong inside the verification boundary — and what remains outside it.

03

Run the pilot

Apply the relevant DigiEmu verification infrastructure to the selected workflow and capture the resulting evidence trail.

04

Review the evidence

Examine what was recorded, what can be verified and whether the workflow can later be reconstructed from the retained basis.

03 What we need

From you

A small, real workflow.

  • A defined AI-assisted process
  • Representative source material
  • A clear reason the process may need to be reconstructed
  • One responsible contact for scope and review

From us

A concrete verification result.

  • Evidence-boundary definition
  • Traceability and provenance review
  • Verification / reconstruction assessment
  • Concrete evidence package or pilot output
04 Boundaries

What a pilot does not claim.

  • It does not prove the objective truth of an AI-generated statement.
  • It does not certify that a governance or business decision is ethically or legally correct.
  • It cannot reconstruct evidence that was never captured.
  • Verification remains scoped to the explicitly defined evidence boundary.
