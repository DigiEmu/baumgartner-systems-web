# Visit Scheduling Policy — Demo

**Document ID:** POLICY-DEMO-001
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Demonstration only

## Rules
1. A recurring visit may be moved when the requested time does not conflict with a documented care activity.
2. The family or nominated contact should receive confirmation of the new time.
3. The internal scheduling record should be updated before the first changed visit.
4. If the requested time conflicts with a care activity, the request must be escalated for manual review.
5. The evidence record should retain the request, the policy version used, the staff note, and the resulting confirmation.

## Verification boundary
This demo policy is fictional and exists only to demonstrate provenance, reconstruction and verification behavior in Arca.
