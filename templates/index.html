05 / Boundary

Verification is not omniscience.

We can verify recorded evidence, lineage, transformations and system state. We do not claim to prove:

  • objective truth of an AI output
  • correctness of model internal reasoning
  • ethical correctness of a governance decision
  • existence of evidence that was never recorded

Trust starts by knowing exactly what has — and has not — been proven.
