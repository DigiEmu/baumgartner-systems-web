01 / Problem

The problem is not whether AI can make a decision.
The problem is whether you can later explain what happened.

  • What information was available?
  • What changed?
  • Can the decision trail be reconstructed?

Evidence. Provenance. Verification.

02 / Evidence

Make the decision trail reconstructable.

Identity

Know which agents, models, data sources and human reviewers participated in a decision.

Lineage

Record how inputs, versions, parameters and context flowed into each step.

State

Capture the system state, configuration and environment at the point of decision.

Provenance

Preserve the origin, chain of custody and transformations applied to every artefact.

Verification

Enable independent reconstruction and cross-checking of the recorded decision trail.

05 / Boundary

Verification is not omniscience.

We can verify recorded evidence, lineage, transformations and system state. We do not claim to prove:

  • objective truth of an AI output
  • correctness of model internal reasoning
  • ethical correctness of a governance decision
  • existence of evidence that was never recorded

Trust starts by knowing exactly what has — and has not — been proven.
