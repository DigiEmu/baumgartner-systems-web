01 Independent Verification

Verification of evidence, not of claims.

We examine what a system actually recorded: where evidence came from, whether it remained intact and whether the recorded result can be independently verified.

Verification is always scoped to an explicitly defined boundary — what was captured, under which conditions and within which system state. What lies outside that boundary is stated, not assumed.

  • Evidence provenance and chain-of-custody review
  • Integrity and consistency checks on recorded artifacts
  • Structured, independently examinable verification results
  • Reconstruction assessment within the agreed boundary
02 Technical Evaluation & Pilots

Scoped evaluation before any commitment.

A technical evaluation is bounded before work begins: one workflow, an agreed set of acceptance criteria and testing on synthetic or representative material.

The outcome is an independent report describing methods, results and limitations — designed to be reviewed by your own technical and governance stakeholders.

  • Scope and acceptance criteria agreed in advance
  • Testing on synthetic or representative material
  • Independent reporting of methods, results and limitations
  • No obligation to proceed beyond the evaluation
03 Integration & Interoperability

Integration that preserves the evidence trail.

Verification infrastructure only helps if it fits the systems already in place. We define integration boundaries explicitly and keep evidence portable across systems and formats.

Verification workflows are shaped around existing processes rather than replacing them — including an honest account of what remains outside the boundary.

  • Explicit definition of integration and verification boundaries
  • Evidence portability across systems and formats
  • Verification workflows aligned with existing processes
  • Documentation of what remains outside the boundary
04 Enterprise Licensing & Support

Licensing and support, scoped per deployment.

Where an evaluation leads to adoption, licensing, integration support and maintenance are negotiated individually — matched to the systems, boundaries and verification requirements actually in use.

We do not publish a price list. Terms are agreed per engagement and reflect the scope that was actually verified, not a generic tier.

  • Individually negotiated licensing arrangements
  • Integration support and ongoing maintenance
  • Scope limited to agreed systems and verification boundaries
  • Terms agreed per engagement; no published price list
05 Engagement Model

A possible path, not a commitment.

Discovery → Scoped Evaluation → Paid Integration → Ongoing Support
01

Discovery

A short discussion of the workflow, the evidence problem and whether a scoped evaluation is worth running.

02

Scoped Evaluation

A bounded technical evaluation with agreed acceptance criteria and independent reporting of results.

03

Paid Integration

If the results justify it, integration of verification infrastructure under individually negotiated terms.

04

Ongoing Support

Maintenance and support for the integrated scope, as agreed per engagement.

These stages describe a possible commercial path, not a commitment. Each stage is optional, separately scoped and separately agreed — an enquiry or a discovery discussion creates no obligation on either side.
